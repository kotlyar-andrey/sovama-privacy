Sovama Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Sovama is a math practice app for children developed by Andrey Kotlyar. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data collection

Sovama does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Data on your device

Learning progress, settings and an optional name or nickname are stored only on your device. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, this data may be included in your device backup to your Google account. The backup is managed by Google, and we cannot access it.

Certificates and sharing

The app can create certificate images, which may show the name or nickname entered in the app. These images are saved to your device's gallery. They are shared only when you choose to share them yourself through the Android share menu.

Permissions

The app does not request any sensitive permissions.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and to comply with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not collect any personal information from children. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us at the email below.

Deleting your data

You can delete all app data at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Certificate images saved to the gallery stay there after you uninstall the app, and you can delete them in your gallery app.

Changes

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
